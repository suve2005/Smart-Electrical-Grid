                        Governing Mathematical Equations in the Twin
                    

                    

                        

                            
1. Bidirectional Branch Voltage Drop / Rise ($\Delta V_\phi$)

                            

                                V_{k,\phi} = V_{k-1,\phi} - [ R_{line}·(P_{load,\phi} - P_{PV,\phi}) + X_{line}·(Q_{load,\phi} \mp Q_{inv,\phi} - Q_{cap}) ] / V_{nom}
                            

                            
When rooftop solar export $P_{PV,\phi} > P_{load,\phi}$, power flows backward toward the transformer and raises $V_{k,\phi}$ above $230\text{V}$.

                        


                        

                            
2. Phase Imbalance & Neutral Wire Current ($I_N$)

                            

                                I_N = | I_R∠0° + I_Y∠-120° + I_B∠+120° | ,   VUF(%) = (max|V_\phi - V_{avg}| / V_{avg}) × 100
                            

                            
In a balanced 3-phase grid, $I_N = 0\text{ A}$. Single-phase houses and solar panels skew $I_R, I_Y, I_B$, driving high current through the neutral wire.

                        


                        

                            
3. Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) Virtual Sensing

                            

                                L_{total} = λ_1 ||V_{ESP32} - \hat{V}||^2 + λ_2 ||\hat{I}_{bus} - Y_{bus}·\hat{V}||^2
                            

                            
Infers exact voltages at unmonitored intermediate poles (`Bus A2`, `Bus B1`) using only sparse ESP32 sensors + Kirchhoff's Current Law.
